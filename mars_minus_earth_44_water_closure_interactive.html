Model and evidence

89 frames from 4.4 Ga to present in 50 Myr steps. Conductive geotherms include isotope decay and assumed basal cooling. Mars uses literature-informed 20–25 km closure endmembers, temperature-dependent viscous removal and depth-dependent mineral occlusion; Earth uses 10–20 km effective connected-domain caps and thermal closure, not a universal measured pore boundary. Water conservation: initial + outgassed = unbound + mineral bound + escaped. Mineral-bound water does not count as habitable liquid.

Green: Mars ≥90% scenario maximum. Grey band: LUCA clock estimate 4.09–4.33 Ga; dotted line: 4.2 Ga (Moody et al. 2024), not the date of abiogenesis. Mean water temperature is a modeled volume-weighted mean over 0–80 °C assigned pore water. Historical Tharsis curve is extracted unchanged from the earlier project, covers only 4.0–0 Ga, and represents a scenario reservoir rather than total Mars water. It is not added to the new budget. Source citations, parameters and uncertainty definitions accompany the manuscript and archive.

The thermal and water endmembers do not establish Martian life, an earlier origin than Earth, universal ancestral thermophily, Venus inoculation or common ancestry between planets. The Bayesian model separates physical transfer counts from assumed survival and establishment probabilities.
